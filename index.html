Mechanics project · 2024

Oval vs. Round
Bicycle Chainrings

To compare oval and round chainrings, I simulated the pedalling cycle across pedal angles to examine how chainring geometry affects crank torque through a revolution. Two experiments complemented the model: one measured the torque needed to lift a load at different pedal angles, and another estimated transmitted work from wheel rotations. The simulation suggested only marginal differences under the conditions studied.

ML project · 2026

Toxic Gas
Identification

Can sensor readings predict gas concentrations across different humidity conditions? I built a multi-output regression pipeline, engineered humidity-related features, and compared random validation with a high-humidity holdout to assess generalization. The holdout exposed a substantial performance drop under distribution shift.

School project · 3 months · 2025

SmartRoute

As team lead, I worked with a five-student team to build an MTB route planner. We used Strava heatmap data to estimate trail popularity and OpenStreetMap to build the route network, then applied weighted Dijkstra routing to favor popular trails or explore less-traveled segments. My contributions covered the project’s data and routing components; a teammate built the frontend.
